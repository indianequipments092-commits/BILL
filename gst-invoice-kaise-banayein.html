GST Invoice Kaise Banayein

GST invoice sirf bill nahi, tax ka kaanooni dastavez hai. Galat ya adhura invoice hone par buyer ko Input Tax Credit milne me dikkat aa sakti hai. Yahan seedhe tareeke se samjhein.

Invoice me kya-kya hona chahiye

  • Seller ka naam, pata aur GSTIN
  • Invoice number (ek hi financial year me unique) aur date
  • Buyer ka naam, pata aur GSTIN (registered ho to)
  • Item ka description, HSN/SAC code, quantity aur unit
  • Discount ke baad taxable value
  • Tax rate aur amount: CGST + SGST, ya IGST
  • Place of supply (state ke naam ke saath) aur total amount
  • Seller ke signature / digital signature

CGST + SGST ya IGST?

Same state me supply ho to tax do hisso me banta hai: CGST (centre) aur SGST (state), barabar-barabar. Doosre state me supply par poora tax IGST ke roop me lagta hai.

Udaharan: ₹10,000 ka maal, 18% GST.
Same state: CGST ₹900 + SGST ₹900 = total ₹11,800.
Doosra state: IGST ₹1,800 = total ₹11,800.

Step-by-step

  1. Apni company aur GSTIN details ek baar bharein.
  2. Party chunein ya nayi jodein (GSTIN ke saath).
  3. Items daalein — HSN aur GST rate sahi chunein.
  4. Supply ka state dekhkar CGST/SGST ya IGST check karein.
  5. Invoice save karke PDF share karein.

IE Billing me ye sab auto hota hai: GST khud calculate hota hai, aur invoice bhejte hi doosri party ke paas Purchase Bill ban jaata hai.
